# Acceptance Checklist

Use this when translating a brief into scope.

## Clarify

- Who is the actor?
- What observable behavior should change?
- What must remain unchanged?
- What would count as failure?

## Surface

- explicit goals
- non-goals
- empty, error, and edge cases
- rollout or migration constraints
- external dependencies

## Output Shape

- problem statement
- acceptance criteria
- non-goals
- edge cases
- risky assumptions
- suggested packet boundaries
